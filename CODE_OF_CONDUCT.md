# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Garuda's community to be open and welcoming to everyone, whatever their age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and speak in ways that keep the
community friendly, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Good behaviour:

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree.
- Respect other opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to the people affected, and learn from them.
- Think of what is best for the whole community, not only for yourself.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities of the maintainers

The maintainers explain and enforce these standards. They act in a fair way when they see
behaviour that is not acceptable. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and they give the reasons when it
is right to do so.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and the website. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for
example from an official account or at an event.

## Report a problem

Send a report to [garuda.codeofconduct@gmail.com](mailto:garuda.codeofconduct@gmail.com). The maintainers review every report quickly and fairly, and keep
the reporter's identity and the details private. For a security problem in Garuda itself, use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on the consequence of a breach. In order of strength:

1. **Correction.** For unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour: a private, written warning that
   explains the problem. A public apology can be requested.
2. **Warning.** For one serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences if the
   behaviour goes on. No contact with the people involved for a set time, including unrequested
   contact with the maintainers.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a ban from
   all interaction with the community for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of a person, or aggression toward or
   disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from all interaction with the community.
